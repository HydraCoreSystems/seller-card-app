## Full description (4,000 characters at most)

> Sale night, without the mess.
>
> SprigList is a light helper for plant sellers who run sales in Facebook groups. Save your seller details once, keep a simple plant list, and get every card ready to paste. When the comments roll in, it sorts out who claimed first. When the sale is over, it helps you collect.
>
> WHO CLAIMED FIRST, SETTLED
> Paste the comment thread and SprigList lists the claims on every lot in thread order. Ties are marked plainly so you make the call, not the app. The rundown shows each winner, who's next in line, every buyer's total, and the questions still waiting on an answer.
>
> SECOND CHANCE
> When a winner doesn't pay inside the window you set, offer the lot to the next in line at the same price with one tap. The message is written for you. Dead claims turn back into sales.
>
> THE MONEY CHASE
> Every buyer still owing has a polite reminder ready to copy or send, with their lots, their total, your shipping and your payment handles filled in. Mark them paid and the list gets shorter.
>
> SEASON TOTALS
> Plants sold, money taken, shipping collected and what's still owed, by week, month and season. One tap makes a clean file for your tax preparer or your own spreadsheet.
>
> PRICE MEMORY
> Selling a plant you've sold before? The lot quietly shows what it went for last time, and when.
>
> CARDS AND PHOTOS
> Your announcement and every lot's card, built from your saved profile so you never type your shipping or payment terms again. Plant photos get a slim stamp with your name and the date. Your original photos are never changed.
>
> THE SALE SHEET
> Rather type your lots at a keyboard? Export a Sale Sheet, fill it in Google Sheets or Excel, and bring it back. The lots build in order, and any row that can't be read is pointed out before anything changes.
>
> LEFTOVERS AND BUYER MEMORY
> Unclaimed and unpaid lots gather into one list, ready to relist in a tap. Returning buyers are recognized, and you can keep a short note on each.
>
> YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR PHONE
> No account, no sign in. Your plants, sales and records live on your phone.
>
> YOU STAY IN CHARGE
> SprigList gets everything ready. You post it yourself, in your own words, where you choose.
>
> Try the sample showcase first: one tap shows every screen full, with made up names, and nothing you do there is saved.
>
> Made by Gathering Moss, a plant seller who got tired of sorting claims at midnight.

(About 2,380 characters.)
